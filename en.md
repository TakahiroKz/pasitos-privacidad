# Pasitos Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

## 1. Introduction and controller

Pasitos is an app for tracking a baby’s sleep and routine. It is designed to be used **exclusively by parents or legal guardians**, that is, by adults. It is not directed at minors.

This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored and who it is shared with. The data controller is the developer of Pasitos. For any question you can write to contacto@capitalfinaura.com.

## 2. Dual storage: local or cloud

**Local mode (no sign-up).** If you do not sign in, your baby’s data is stored strictly in your device’s internal storage. The developer does not collect, access or back up this information. Even so, from time to time the app asks Google (Firebase Remote Config) for some operating settings; that request uses an installation identifier of your device and never includes your baby’s data.

**Synced mode (with an account).** If you optionally choose to sign in with Google Sign-In, your data is synced and stored in the cloud so you can see it on other devices. Nothing is synced without signing in. With Premium you can also **share a baby** with other people (up to 4 in total, including you): those who accept your invitation, who must sign in with Google, can see and record that baby’s data and see the name they use in the app. Only the person who created the baby can invite and remove people.

## 3. Data collected

**From the adult** (obtained through Google OAuth, only if you sign in): your name and email address. The app also asks for a name to greet you. If you have Premium and are signed in, to confirm your subscription the app may send our server (Firebase) the purchase identifier assigned by Google Play; this is not your payment data.

**From the child** (entered by the adult): the baby’s name or alias, date of birth, weeks of gestation at birth (optional) and routine records: wake-up time, start and end of naps, and bedtime.

**Device permissions and connection:** notifications permission and, optionally, exact alarms permission, to show the nap reminders you set up (notifications are generated on your device); Internet access, to sync, read the settings and show ads; and the advertising identifier permission, used by the ads SDK.

## 4. Third-party service providers

**Authentication, database and settings: Firebase** (a Google LLC service). Firebase Authentication verifies your Google account, Cloud Firestore stores your data in the cloud, encrypted in transit and at rest, Cloud Functions may confirm your Premium subscription and Remote Config delivers the operating settings. Only your account and the people you choose to share a baby with can read your data, through access rules.

**Advertising (freemium model): Google AdMob.** The free version may show ads, never on the Today logging screen, in reminders or in the widget. To show them, AdMob may process your device’s advertising identifier, your IP address (which gives an approximate location), your interactions with ads and diagnostic data. The app is directed at adults, not at minors: ads are always non-personalized and have content suitable for all audiences, and where the law requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) your consent is requested before they are shown. The Premium version shows no ads.

**Payment processing (Premium): Google Play Billing**, which manages the monthly or yearly subscription. The app **does not store or access** any bank or credit card details; payment is processed solely by Google Play.

## 5. Children’s privacy (COPPA and local laws)

The app **does not collect data directly from minors** and is not directed at them: its users are parents or legal guardians, who voluntarily provide all the information about the baby. We do not use that information for anything other than showing it to you, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

## 6. Data deletion and user rights

You can **delete your account and all associated data** in two ways: from the app, under “My account” > “Delete my account”, or by writing to contacto@capitalfinaura.com. The steps are also at https://takahirokz.github.io/pasitos-privacidad/eliminar-cuenta/. When you delete it, we remove from the cloud your identifier, name, email and your children’s data, and we do not keep copies or apply any additional retention period. You can also delete a child from “My family”, correct their data at any time, and sign out whenever you want. If you share a baby, whoever leaves or is removed stops seeing it; if the person who created the baby deletes the baby or their account, it is deleted for everyone. A baby that someone else shared with you belongs to the person who created it: for your name to stop appearing on it, leave it first from “My family”.

Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. If you only use local mode, there is no copy on our servers. You can exercise your rights of access, rectification and erasure by writing to contacto@capitalfinaura.com. If this policy changes, we will let you know in the app and ask you to accept it again.
