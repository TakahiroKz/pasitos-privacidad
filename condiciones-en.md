Every baby is different: not all babies go through the same stages or at the same age. If you are worried about your baby's health or sleep, talk to your pediatrician. If you share a baby, you choose with whom; the person who created the baby is the one who can invite and remove people, and invited people can view and record data for that baby. Share only with people you trust.

You can delete your account and data at any time, from "My account" > "Delete my account" or by writing to contacto@capitalfinaura.com (steps at https://takahirokz.github.io/pasitos-privacidad/eliminar-cuenta/). Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.
